The service

OneJump provides software for coaches to program training, communicate with clients, collect payments and get AI-drafted suggestions (“Assist”). Coaches stay responsible for the coaching they provide and for every message they send, including ones that started as Assist drafts.

Accounts

Keep your password safe and turn on two-step login. You’re responsible for activity in your account. Coaches must have permission to add clients and their information.

Plans and billing

New workspaces get a 14-day Pro trial without a card. Paid plans are billed monthly through Stripe and renew until cancelled. You can cancel any time; your plan stays active until the end of the period. Client payments go to the coach’s own Stripe account and OneJump takes no cut.

Acceptable use

Don’t use OneJump to send spam, harass anyone, give medical diagnoses, or upload content you don’t have rights to. Don’t try to access other businesses’ data.

AI suggestions

Assist suggestions can be wrong. They’re drafts for a coach to review, not professional, medical or legal advice.

Your data

Coaches own their business data and clients own their personal data. See the privacy policy for details. You can export your data at any time.

Changes and contact

We’ll email coaches about material changes at least 14 days before they apply. Questions: hello@onejumpfit.com.
