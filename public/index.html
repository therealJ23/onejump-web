Questions

How much time will I really save?

Most coaches lose the most time reading check-ins and writing replies. Assist writes the first draft, so you only check and approve. OneJump counts the minutes it saves you and shows the total every week.

Does Assist message my clients on its own?

No. Anything Assist writes waits for your OK. Only fixed reminders you switch on, like “your check-in is due”, send by themselves.

Is the AI going to replace me?

No. Assist does the typing and the sorting; you make the decisions. Clients pay for you, and OneJump gives you more time for them.

Do my clients have to count calories?

Only if you want them to. For each client you pick habits, targets or a meal plan, and you can hide the numbers completely.

What happens with injuries or medical questions?

Assist never diagnoses or gives medical advice. It flags the message to you and drafts a reply that suggests seeing a qualified professional.

Who owns my client data?

You do. Export it any time. OneJump never contacts or markets to your clients.

What does it cost my clients?

Nothing. The client app is free for them. You charge them through your own Stripe account, and OneJump takes no cut.
