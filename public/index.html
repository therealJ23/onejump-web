Questions

Does Assist message my clients on its own?

Only fixed reminders you switch on, like “your check-in is due”. Anything Assist writes itself waits for your approval.

Is the AI going to replace me?

No. Assist drafts and organizes; you decide. Clients pay for your judgment and your relationship with them, and OneJump gives you more time for both.

What happens with injuries or medical questions?

Assist doesn’t diagnose. When a client mentions pain, injury or a medical condition, it flags it to you and drafts a reply that suggests seeing a qualified professional.

Who owns my client data?

You do. Export it any time. OneJump never contacts, markets to or recruits your clients.

What does it cost my clients?

Nothing. The client app is free for them. You charge them through your own Stripe account, and OneJump takes no cut.
